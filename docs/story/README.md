# Story mode

**Status:** the framework and boss 1 (Schiff) are built (Oct 7, 2026). Story is hidden until the other four bosses are in: open **proofoffight.com/?story** once and the 📖 Story button shows up on that phone or computer from then on. Prompts for every image and song are below.

## The story

You pick a founder. In Free play the founders fight each other; in Story they team up, because the naysayers are coming. You fight five bosses in a set order, the other founders are your assists, and Satoshi has your back once.

| # | Boss | Stage | Why they're here |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Peter Schiff** | A gold vault | "Buy gold." The warm-up. Personal if you're Saylor. |
| 2 | **Jamie Dimon** | A marble bank lobby with a pet rock in a museum case | Called Bitcoin a "pet rock" and said he'd close it down. |
| 3 | **Elizabeth Warren** | A Senate hearing room | Said she was building an anti-crypto army. |
| 4 | **Sam Bankman-Fried** | A prison yard, his Corolla parked outside the fence | The insider who handed the other three their best argument. |
| 5 | **WICK** | Inside the chart | The market itself. |

**The twist:** you beat the gold bug, the banker, the regulator and the fraud, and then the chart shows up. WICK isn't anyone. He is what 100x leverage does to people. The lesson is in his rules, not in a lecture (below).

Every boss fight gets its own **portrait, sprite, stage background and song**, plus buttons and a Super like a fighter's kit.

## The mechanics

**Assists.** Before each boss you pick an ally from the other founders. When your HP drops to 35% or lower, an **ASSIST** button lights up, once per fight: the ally slides in, hits for 20 that can't be dodged or blocked, says their line and leaves. It doesn't use your turn. Some ally and boss pairs get their own line (Saylor against Schiff: `THERE IS NO SECOND BEST`).

**Satoshi, once per run.** When you're knocked out, he shimmers in (sliced steel, like the Lugano statue), you get back up with half your HP, and the fight goes on. He says `STAND UP. WE'RE ALL COUNTING ON YOU.` and vanishes with his real last words: `I'VE MOVED ON TO OTHER THINGS.` Once he's used, a loss shows **CONTINUE?** with a 10-second countdown, which restarts that boss fight. Continues are counted on your result.

**Getting harder.** Each boss has more HP than the last and a smarter CPU. The assists and Satoshi offset it. Target: a sensible player beats Schiff about 70% of the time and WICK about 50%, before help.

**Saving.** Close the tab mid-run and pick up where you left off, like the Daily Fight.

**The ending.** After WICK falls, Satoshi appears one last time, then a shareable result: `Beat story mode with Toly · 5 bosses · peak leverage 3x · 1 continue`.

### WICK: the lesson is in the rules

- **Leverage meter.** Every pink or purple button you press against him adds leverage: 1x, 2x, 5x, 10x, 25x, 50x, 100x. It shows on your HUD.
- **Liquidation Cascade** (his Super) hits harder the more leverage you've stacked. At 1x it's a nudge; at 100x it's the end.
- **Stop Hunt** goes straight through Privacy: `YOUR STOP LOSS WAS A SUGGESTION`.
- Red and blue never add leverage. Steady play beats him; degen play feeds him.
- Lines: `100X? CUTE.` When he loses: `SEE YOU SUNDAY, 3 A.M.`
- Satoshi's last word, after WICK: `IT WAS NEVER THE BANKERS. IT WAS THE 100X.` (draft)
- Your **peak leverage** goes on the share card, so people can brag about a 1x run.

### Sam Bankman-Fried: background

- Serving 25 years at a low-security federal prison in California, projected release 2044. The Second Circuit upheld the conviction in June 2026; a Supreme Court petition (Sept 2026) and a pardon request are pending, and the Senate voted unanimously against a pardon in July.
- The car was a **Toyota Corolla**. He drove it as a billionaire, and it was part of the "normal guy" image; at trial, Caroline Ellison testified the company cars were downgraded because it looked better for FTX.
- Kit ideas (to draft): `FTX IS FINE. ASSETS ARE FINE.`, `PARDON PENDING`, the beanbag he slept on, and a mechanic where his hits take your Blocks as his own (customer funds).

## What's in the game (Oct 7, 2026)

- **📖 Story** on the title screen (after `?story`), or **Resume story · Boss N** when a run is saved.
- **Pick your fighter**, then the **ladder**: a tower with the final boss on top. Rungs whose boss isn't built yet say *coming soon*, and the final boss is `???` until you reach it.
- **Pick your ally** before every boss. A ⭐ marks an ally with their own line against that boss.
- **The fight:** the boss's stage colors (their background art replaces them once it's made), the boss's HP, and your ally's face in the top-left corner. It lights up gold at 35% HP: tap it (or press A) and they slide in, shout their line, and hit for 20. An assist can land the knockout.
- **Knocked out with Satoshi unused:** he turns into view, `STAND UP.` / `WE'RE ALL COUNTING ON YOU.`, turns away with `I'VE MOVED ON TO OTHER THINGS.`, and you're back up with half your HP. Until his art is made he's drawn as a sliced-steel hooded figure with a laptop.
- **Losing after that:** **CONTINUE?** with a 10-second countdown. Continue restarts that boss fight (a coin goes in); Give up or zero ends the run with a **GAME OVER** card you can share.
- **Winning:** a **BOSS DOWN** card (the boss's loser line, share, Post on X, Next boss). After the final boss, Satoshi's ending lines, then **STORY COMPLETE**.
- **Saving:** the run is saved between fights. Leaving mid-fight keeps your place on the ladder; that fight starts over.
- **Music:** each boss plays their own song once it's added; until then, the fight song.

**Balance** (`node tools/story-sim.js`, a sensible player): Schiff, 110 HP, is beaten 65% of the time with no help, 80% with the assist, and 98% with the assist and Satoshi.

**Where it lives:** the bosses and `STORY` settings in `js/data.js`; `assist()` and `revive()` in `js/engine.js`; the ladder, ally pick, Satoshi and the after-fight cards in `js/app.js` (search "Story mode"); styles in `css/style.css` (search "Story mode").
