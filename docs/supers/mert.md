# Mert Super — CEO of Helium

**Game:** Proof of Fight  
**Fighter:** Mert (`mert`)  
**Status:** Flavor lock for v1. Replaces **Helius Flare** as the name people see.  
**Job in the kit:** control Super. Small hit + **Blind** (skip next turn). Dome stays the picture. Wrong company stays the joke.

He already runs this bit. He has posted that between being “ceo of helium” and “every bald guy ever,” nobody knows his true identity, and that people confuse Helius for Helium every day (the reverse only happened once, which he treated as making it). Third name in the flicker: Hivemapper, which he pairs with Helium when explaining crypto to normies. Do not use official Helius, Helium, or Hivemapper logos.

## Why these slots

**Name card: `CEO OF HELIUM`**  
Wrong company on purpose. Name card flickers once before it locks: `HELIUS` → `HELIUM` → `HIVEMAPPER` → `CEO OF HELIUM`. That is the two-or-three-projects gag without putting three names on the button.

**Voice line: `THE RPCS DID THIS`**  
Under 40 characters. It is the standing joke that every outage, launch, and crime coin is his RPC’s fault. He has pretended to quit over it.

**KO: `TRILLIONS`**  
His closer. Short enough for a share card. Optimism as a haymaker.

**Effect: Blind**  
Same job as the old Helius Flare. Sun off the dome, they cannot see who hit them, they skip the next turn. The identity gag and the Blind are the same joke. 8 damage so it is not a free skip with no cost.

**Prop: ☀️ on the cut-in, 🎈 on the KO**  
Dome beam during the slam. Loser is left holding a helium balloon with the wrong logo scratched out. Result card: squinting, balloon string in one hand.

**CPU after: `THE PLAN IS WORKING`**  
His line after admitting the Helium / bald-guy cover identity.

**Rival KO vs Garlinghouse: `9/11 FOR XRP MFERS`**  
His actual Western Union line. Optional. Cut it if the banner feels too sharp; fallback is still `TRILLIONS`.

## Build notes (Oct 1, 2026): what's in the game

Added when this was built into the game. Everything above is the original concept, unedited.

**Super, built as written:** `CEO OF HELIUM` name card that flickers `HELIUS` → `HELIUM` → `HIVEMAPPER` before it locks, sun off the dome (sun rays plus a beam sweeping toward the opponent), voice line `THE RPCS DID THIS`, 8 damage + Blind, KO line `TRILLIONS`, loser left holding a 🎈, CPU line `THE PLAN IS WORKING`.

**Mert is the first fighter with his own button names.** Same colors, jobs and odds as everyone else:

| Button | Name | Lands | Flops |
|---|---|---|---|
| Red (reliable hit) | 🗯️ Shitpost | no banner | can't fail |
| Blue (dodge, 45%) | 🕶️ Zolana | `EVERY BALD GUY EVER` | `VIEW KEY LEAKED` |
| Pink (medium gamble, 45%) | 🪙 Memecoin | `I LIKE MEMECOINS` | `SNIPED IN BLOCK ZERO` |
| Purple (big gamble, 40%) | ⛔ Rate Limit | `429: TOO MANY REQUESTS` | `STATUS PAGE: ALL GREEN` |

**Changed from the concept above:**
- `ZOLANA` is the blue button's name instead of its success banner. The success banner is `EVERY BALD GUY EVER`: he vanishes into the crowd of bald guys.
- The pink button is Memecoin, so its fail line is `SNIPED IN BLOCK ZERO` instead of `RIGHT-CLICK SAVED` (an NFT joke).
- The purple button is Rate Limit (his real job: cutting off your RPC) instead of keeping `DEV WALLET WAS A DECOY`. You can't hide from a rate limit, which is why it hits through dodges.
- `WHO WAS THAT` shows over the opponent on the turn they skip, with a 🙈 over their head (stand-in for "they cover their eyes").
- Head emoji is now 👨‍🦲 (the dome); ☀️ is the prop.
- **Cut:** the rival KO line vs Garlinghouse (no 9/11 jokes on share cards; TRILLIONS is used instead until there's a replacement) and the CPU timing preference (the CPU always fires its Super at 10 Blocks).

**Balance note:** 8 damage instead of 10 matters more than it looks. Strike does 10, so a 10-damage Super lines up exactly with the KO line, and at 8 a Strike-only Mert needs an extra turn (simulator: 64% → 51% for Strike-only). For players who mix moves he barely changes (72% → 71%) and stays mid-pack.

**Where it lives:** `js/data.js` (`mert.moves`, `mert.super`), cut-in in `js/app.js` (`case 'helium'`, name-card flicker in `cutIn`) and `css/style.css` (search "CEO of Helium").
